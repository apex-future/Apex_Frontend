# Apex App — Subscription Plans

This document outlines the four subscription tiers for the Apex application, designed with affordability for Nigerian students in mind. Each paid plan offers a monthly and a "semester" (3-month) billing cycle, with the semester plan offering a discount to encourage longer commitments.

> **Format:** Features are listed first, then access level across each tier is specified. This gives a precise picture of what each tier gets for every feature.

---

## Pricing Overview

| Tier | Monthly | Semester (3 Months) |
|---|---|---|
| **Freemium** | N0 | N0 |
| **Apex Mini** | N1,000 | N2,500 *(Save N500)* |
| **Apex Plus** | N2,000 | N5,000 *(Save N1,000)* |
| **Apex Premium** | N3,500 | N9,000 *(Save N1,500)* |

### Sync and Storage

Syncing is intentionally NOT a paywall feature. Locking sync would also limit in-app sharing, restrict analytics, and create friction that hurts us as much as the user. Sync stays free for all logged-in users.

| Feature | Freemium | Apex Mini | Apex Plus | Apex Premium |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Open Questions / To Decide

- **"Advanced Dictionary":** Feature is not built yet. When it is, decide what it covers (translation, etymology, bilingual support) before assigning it to a tier.
- **Book import limit:** No cap for any tier for now. Revisit if storage costs become a concern.

# Apex App - Subscription Plans

This document outlines the four subscription tiers for the Apex application, designed with affordability for Nigerian students in mind. Each paid plan offers a monthly and a "semester" (3-month) billing cycle, with the semester plan offering a discount to encourage longer commitments.

## 1. Freemium (Free)
Designed for casual users who need basic reading and study tools.

*   **Pricing:** ₦0
*   **Features:**
    *   Basic Book Reader (Standard formats)
    *   Basic Dictionary access
    *   Limited Notes (Up to 5 notes)
    *   1 Notebook limit
    *   Limited Flashcards (Up to 20 per day)
    *   Ad-supported experience
    *   Local storage only

## 2. Apex Mini
An affordable entry-level plan for students who need more robust note-taking and an ad-free experience.

*   **Pricing:**
    *   **Monthly:** ₦1,000
    *   **Semester (3 Months):** ₦2,500 *(Save ₦500)*
*   **Features:**
    *   *All Freemium features, plus:*
    *   Ad-free experience
    *   Unlimited Notes
    *   Up to 5 Notebooks
    *   Unlimited Flashcards
    *   Study Reminders
    *   Basic Cloud Sync (Single device backup)

## 3. Apex Plus
The ideal plan for dedicated students requiring advanced study tools and cross-device syncing.

*   **Pricing:**
    *   **Monthly:** ₦2,000
    *   **Semester (3 Months):** ₦5,000 *(Save ₦1,000)*
*   **Features:**
    *   *All Apex Mini features, plus:*
    *   Advanced Reader Tools (Highlighting, annotations, bookmarks)
    *   Unlimited Notebooks
    *   Access to Quizzes
    *   Basic Study Wrap (Weekly study stats)
    *   Cross-device Cloud Sync (Up to 3 devices)

## 4. Apex Premium
The ultimate study companion featuring advanced insights and unlimited capabilities.

*   **Pricing:**
    *   **Monthly:** ₦3,500
    *   **Semester (3 Months):** ₦9,000 *(Save ₦1,500)*
*   **Features:**
    *   *All Apex Plus features, plus:*
    *   Advanced Dictionary & Translation tools
    *   Detailed Study Wrap (In-depth analytics & term insights)
    *   AI-powered Book Summaries and Study Guides
    *   Export Notes & Flashcards to PDF/Word
    *   Unlimited Cloud Storage (Sync across unlimited devices)
    *   Priority Customer Support
